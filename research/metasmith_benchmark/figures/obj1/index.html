metasmith benchmark · objective 1 · E1 vs E2 on CAMI II

Does metasmith reproduce nf-core/mag?

E1 ran nf-core/mag 5.5.0 on 249 CAMI II samples: 208 short-read and 41 nanopore. E2 ran the same pipeline as a plan metasmith solved from a library of the same tool calls.

1 · workflow

The solved plan is nf-core's pipeline, step for step

Steps only: each data node is folded into the step that produces it. Left, nextflow's own DAG of the E1 run, cut to the processes that ran on that arm. Right, the plan metasmith solved for E2, which is the plan that ran. All four are drawn by metasmith's DAG renderer at the same scale, and a step's function has the same colour in each. The full graphs, with their data, are at the end of the page. The plan's two scoring steps against the CAMI gold standard are left out, because nf-core has no counterpart to them.

# Writing style: earlier sections vs the Discussion (2026-11-25)

Tony's register, taken from the Introduction (except the automated-composition paragraph, which was drafted by Claude) and the Results.

| Feature | Tony (Intro, Results) | Discussion as drafted |
|---|---|---|
| Sentence links | "since" for cause ("This is problematic since..."), "so", "such that", "thereby"; sentence-initial "However," | "because", mid-sentence "however", "therefore", "namely", "moreover", "in effect", "by contrast" |
| Punctuation | Mostly commas and full stops; colons rare, semicolons almost absent | Colons and semicolons in most sentences, often two clauses hung on one colon |
| Back-reference | Short "This ..." sentences that pick up the last point ("This is done through automation", "This tests whether...", "This matters in cases like when...") | Appositives and inverted clauses carry the link ("being local, they cannot...", "where many standards compete, there is...") |
| Figures of speech | Engineering and economic metaphor: "buys accessibility with expressiveness", "spend", "logistical plumbing", "control surface", "lifts ... out of" | Literary contrasts: "less a vocabulary than a principle", "ship the whole factory" (Tony's own image) |
| Sentence shape | Claim, then mechanism, in one or two medium sentences; summary closer "These results show that..." | Dense compound sentences carrying claim, mechanism and example at once |
| Spelling | -ize with British -ll-/-our: "utilizes", "modelling", "behaviour", "catalogue" | Mixed: "specialises", "maximises", "decentralised" |
| Voice | Active "we" for decisions ("We identified that...", "we observed") | Mostly impersonal |

Rewrite rules applied: split colon and semicolon chains into sentences; use "since", "so", "such that" and sentence-initial "However,"; add a short "This ..." link where a clause referred back; reuse the spend/buy metaphor from the Interface section; -ize spellings; keep every paragraph's framing and every sentence's message.
